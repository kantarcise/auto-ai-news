# Owner feedback on the October 4 release

[Structured notes](release-feedback-2026-10-04.json) match all 21 selected articles in the [published release](https://github.com/kantarcise/auto-ai-news/releases/tag/daily-2026-10-04). They record direct owner feedback, not independent fact-checking or article inspection by the tool. Notes are lightly normalized, not verbatim quotations. No numeric importance or binary inclusion labels were invented.

## Editorial preferences established

- Firsthand accounts and experience posts are valuable, including AI safety bootcamp accounts. News need not be a model launch to deserve inclusion.
- Practical technical depth matters: implementation details, tradeoffs, methods and concrete lessons. The Airbnb account, AutoSynthData and the technical Databricks retail article are positive examples.
- Product/API updates can be useful. The Cloudflare API/search/workspace articles were liked; being published by a vendor is not itself a reason to reject an article.
- Promotional framing should reduce preference when little technical substance is apparent. The NVIDIA samples/skills posts, Genie Agents advice and Vercel customer story drew this concern. These are qualitative negatives, not blanket exclusions or factual ad classifications.
- Mixed judgments stay mixed: the Hugging Face AstaBrief post seemed promotional but acceptable. “Fine” is not equivalent to “essential.”
- Useful synthesis/roundups are welcome, as with the Rundown article.
- Access matters: the Pi edition was reported as apparently paywalled. This is an owner-reported concern, not a verified permanent paywall; HTTP success alone is insufficient proof of readable article content.
- Publisher reputation can be a small prior: the owner expressed confidence in Clever Hack and Anthropic. It should not replace article-level assessment. The Anthropic academy/training announcement was liked despite being corporate news; headline marketing exclusions for the lab bonus must not become blanket rejection rules.
- Group the Hugging Face and AI2 AstaBrief announcements under one story headline, retaining both attributed links when useful. The owner supplied this story relationship explicitly.

The Vercel feedback used a `/customers/` URL, while the release linked a `/blog/` URL with the same article slug/title. Both are retained; redirect/canonical equivalence has not been independently verified.

## How this feeds the evaluation work

This feedback is separate from the blank October 3 candidate batch. It covers only selected articles, so it reveals unwanted inclusions but cannot measure missed important stories. We do not feed tentative assessments directly into the binary precision/recall evaluator or claim quality metrics from them. Importance grades, final inclusion decisions and evidence provenance remain to be agreed for scored labels.
